## Why jewelry remains an easy Christmas gift

Giving clothing or perfume requires knowing a size or a precise taste. Jewelry limits that risk, on one condition: choosing a format that needs no measurement. A ring needs a finger size, a bracelet a wrist size. Earrings fit everyone, which is why they make up most of online jewelers' gift selections.

Three criteria make a good jewelry gift:

- **The material**: 925 silver guarantees a hallmark, whereas an unspecified silver-tone or gold-tone metal guarantees nothing.
- **Simplicity**: a plain stud or a small hoop goes with everything, a very bold design pleases less often.
- **Flexibility**: a design available in silver and gold, or a gift card, leaves a way out if the colour does not suit.

## Jewelry gift ideas by budget

### Under 30 euros: a pair of silver studs

This is the thoughtful-gift budget, for a colleague, a friend or an Advent calendar. Of the 60 pieces in the Nébuleuse Bijoux gift selection, 33 cost less than 30 euros. The brand's fine 925 silver studs have a median of 19 euros, and each design comes in silver or 18-carat gold plating. At Histoire d'Or, the first silver hoops start at 15 euros.

### From 30 to 50 euros: the hoop or the single piece

At this budget, the silver hoop becomes the safe choice. Nébuleuse Bijoux fine 925 silver hoops have a median of 30 euros, and 24 pieces of its gift selection sit between 30 and 50 euros. Agatha offers studs and hoops sold individually in this budget in its Mix & Match range, from 25 euros. To choose between brands on silver, the comparison of [silver earring brands](/en/blog/best-silver-earring-brands/) details prices and designs.

### Over 50 euros: the ready-matched set

Above 50 euros, the gift works better as a set. Nébuleuse Bijoux sells 47 curated sets, groups of several earrings chosen to go together, from 42 to 192 euros, with a median of 75 euros. It is the simplest answer for anyone who wants to give a complete look without matching pieces themselves. The principle is explained in the guide to [matching earring sets](/en/blog/matching-earring-sets-ear-stack/).

## The gift card, the answer when in doubt

The gift card has a bad reputation because it seems impersonal. For jewelry, it is often the fairest choice: earring tastes are very personal, between silver and gold, discreet and visible.

Nébuleuse Bijoux offers an e-gift card in 11 amounts: 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, 90, 100, 120 and 150 euros. Sent by email, it remains possible until the last moment, when delivery times no longer allow a physical gift.

## When to order to receive it before Christmas

Nébuleuse Bijoux states delivery within 48 to 72 hours in mainland France, free from 60 euros. Its shipping policy specifies processing and dispatch within 2 to 5 working days. Two tips follow:

1. **Order by mid-December at the latest** to keep a margin on end-of-year delays.
2. **Keep the gift card as plan B** for a purchase in the last week.

On returns, the buyer has 30 days after receipt to change their mind, on a new and unworn item, return costs paid by the buyer. A gift ordered in early November therefore falls outside this period around Christmas.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Giving a ring without knowing the finger size.** It is the first cause of returns on a jewelry gift.
2. **Choosing a metal without a hallmark.** Only 925 silver or sterling silver guarantee the material.
3. **Betting on a very bold design.** A simple piece gets worn, a flashy one often stays in the box.
4. **Forgetting the packaging.** Jewelry is given in a box, not in its shipping pouch.
