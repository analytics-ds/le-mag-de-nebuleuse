## How this comparison was built

The four brands were selected because they come up most often when looking for a silver earring brand in France. All figures were collected on 25 September 2026 on the official websites: number of silver designs listed online, prices, material stated on the product page. Two other frequently cited brands, Pandora and Thomas Sabo, could not be surveyed that day (website unreachable for the first, silver catalogue not accessible for the second). They are therefore not quantified here.

Four criteria separate the brands:

- **The metal hallmark**: 925 silver stated on the product page, or just a silver look.
- **Range depth**: how many designs are genuinely silver, and in which formats (studs, hoops, drops).
- **Price**: range and median, which says more than the average about where most of the catalogue sits.
- **Finishes**: plain silver, gold plating, or both.

## Nébuleuse Bijoux: the most affordable sterling silver in the panel

Nébuleuse Bijoux is a French brand known for its piercing jewelry, which has built a range of fine 925 silver earrings. Of the 155 designs in stock on 25 September 2026, 76 studs and 61 hoops explicitly state fine 925 silver on the product page, and 135 specify 18-carat gold plating for the gold version.

What sets the brand apart is price positioning. With a median of 19 euros on studs and 30 euros on hoops, sterling silver becomes an everyday purchase rather than an exceptional one. 71 studs out of 86 cost 30 euros or less. The other strength is the dual finish: every stud and hoop comes in silver and in gold, at the same price.

### What the brand states about its terms

- Free delivery from 60 euros, within 48 to 72 hours in mainland France.
- 30 days after receipt to change your mind, return costs paid by the buyer.
- 1-year warranty from delivery on defective items.
- 3 stores to see the designs: Le Marais and the 6th arrondissement in Paris, rue Lafayette in Toulouse.

## Agatha, Histoire d'Or and Marc Orian: three other approaches to silver

### Agatha, the Parisian house of dressier pieces

Agatha, a French jewelry house that claims 50 years of history, lists 78 earrings under its 925/1000 silver filter. On the first catalogue page, prices range from 25 to 120 euros, with many designs around 60 to 95 euros: crafted hoops, multi-piece sets, set studs. Its Mix & Match range sells studs and hoops individually, a useful format for a mismatched ear.

### Histoire d'Or, volume and stores

Histoire d'Or lists 994 earrings under its silver filter and 348 silver hoops, with entry prices at 15 euros. It is the widest choice in the panel, with the advantage of trying pieces in store. On the other hand, the range mixes white silver, rose silver and many cubic zirconia designs, and sorting takes longer to find a simple piece.

### Marc Orian, depth on hoops

Marc Orian lists 303 silver hoops, from 15 euros. It is the brand to check for a very wide choice of diameters in one format. As with Histoire d'Or, the catalogue is large and set pieces take up a significant share.

## Minimalist and timeless silver earrings: what to choose

A timeless piece does not follow the trend of the season. In earrings, three formats play that role:

- **The plain stud**, stone-free, 3 to 6 millimetres, worn every day and never dated.
- **The small thin hoop**, 8 to 15 millimetres in diameter, discreet on the lobe as on a second hole.
- **The clean ring**, without pattern, which pairs with any other jewelry.

At Nébuleuse Bijoux, 48 studs and 44 hoops in sterling silver have no stone, with a median of 19 euros for studs and 32 euros for hoops. That is the base of a minimalist jewelry box for under 100 euros. The ranking of [French minimalist earring brands](/en/blog/best-french-minimalist-earring-brands/) widens the question beyond silver.

### Plain or gold-plated silver for a timeless style

Plain silver looks cool and bright, and matches silver watches and light outfits. 18-carat gold plating warms the complexion and mixes with gold jewelry. Choosing a brand that offers both finishes on the same design makes it possible to build a coherent collection without changing supplier.

## Mistakes to avoid before buying

1. **Confusing silver-tone with silver.** Only 925 silver, sterling silver or 925/1000 guarantee the hallmark.
2. **Ignoring the diameter.** A 12 mm hoop and a 40 mm hoop look nothing alike once worn.
3. **Trusting the entry price.** A 15-euro starting price says nothing about the price of most designs, hence the value of the median.
4. **Forgetting the finish.** A gold and a silver version of the same design do not exist at every brand.
